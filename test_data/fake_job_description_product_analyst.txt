Graduate Product Analyst - BrightPath Digital

BrightPath Digital is hiring a Graduate Product Analyst to help teams understand customer behaviour and improve digital products.

Responsibilities
- Analyse product usage, customer feedback, and operational data to identify opportunities.
- Write SQL queries and build clear dashboards for product and commercial teams.
- Define and monitor product metrics with product managers and engineers.
- Support experiments by documenting hypotheses, success measures, and results.
- Present recommendations clearly to technical and non-technical stakeholders.

Requirements
- Strong analytical thinking demonstrated through study, employment, volunteering, or projects.
- Comfortable working with spreadsheets and at least one data tool such as SQL, Python, or Power BI.
- Clear written and verbal communication.
- Ability to organise work, investigate ambiguous problems, and explain assumptions.
- Interest in digital products and customer experience.

Nice to have
- Experience creating dashboards or reports.
- Familiarity with A/B testing, user research, or agile product development.
- Evidence of teamwork and taking ownership of an improvement.
